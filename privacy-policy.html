Who we are

YCC Compass is operated by Your Certified Consultant ("YCC", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect through the YCC Compass app and website, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account information you provide when you register: name, email address, and role (student, guardian, or counsellor).
  • Profile information used for scholarship and course matching: study level, field of interest, target countries, and test scores you choose to enter.
  • Usage data: pages viewed, features used, and saved items, so the app can restore your progress.
  • Device and advertising identifiers: if you view ad-supported areas of the app, our advertising partner(s) may collect a device advertising ID to serve and measure ads. See "Advertising" below.

How we use this information

  • To operate your account and personalize your scholarship and course matches
  • To let guardians and counsellors view progress you've shared with them
  • To send optional email updates (such as the weekly scholarship digest), only if you opt in
  • To maintain and improve the app, and to keep it secure
  • To serve advertising that helps keep the free tier free (see below)

Advertising

The free tier of YCC Compass may display advertising to support the cost of running the platform. Depending on the ad network used (for example, Google AdMob or Google AdSense), the network may collect your device's advertising identifier and general usage data to select and measure ads, and this may be used for personalized advertising unless you opt out in your device settings.

Premium subscribers do not see ads.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with:

  • Guardians or counsellors you have explicitly connected your account to
  • Service providers who help us run the app (hosting, email delivery, analytics, advertising)
  • Authorities, if required by law

Children's privacy

YCC Compass is intended for students preparing for higher education, typically aged 16 and above, and for their guardians and counsellors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

Your choices

  • You can access, correct, or delete your profile information from within the app at any time.
  • You can opt out of the weekly email digest at any time.
  • You can limit ad personalization through your device's advertising settings.
  • You can request full account deletion by contacting us at the email below.

Data retention and security

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, and take reasonable technical measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be noted on this page with an updated date.
